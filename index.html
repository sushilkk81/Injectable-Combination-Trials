How this list was built

  • Source: ClinicalTrials.gov registry, queried 6 October 2026.
  • Phase 2 or Phase 3, including Phase 1/2 and Phase 2/3 designs.
  • Status: recruiting, not yet recruiting, enrolling by invitation, or active and not recruiting.
  • At least one drug, biological or combination-product intervention given by a parenteral route.
  • A delivery device named in the title, interventions, arm descriptions or study summary. The quoted registry wording is shown under each trial.

Limits to keep in mind

  • Many sponsors do not state the presentation in the registry, so trials that use a pen or autoinjector without saying so are missing. Treat the list as a floor.
  • Device class comes from the wording of the record, not from the regulatory designation of the product.
  • Academic studies of marketed products (for example semaglutide pens) are included because the device is named; filter by sponsor type to separate them.
  • Hospital infusion pumps, drug-eluting stents, inhaled, nasal and oral products were excluded.
  • Titles, drug names and country lists are shortened. Open the NCT link for the full record.
